Delete your Radar account

This page is for the Radar app for Android and web, published by Michał Dakowicz. It explains how to have your account and everything stored under it deleted, what is removed, what is kept, and for how long.

How to request deletion

  1. Export your library first, if you want to keep it. In the app, open Settings → Data → Import & export and export to JSON. Once the account is deleted nothing can be recovered.
  2. Email michal.dakowicz2007@gmail.com from the address on your Radar account, with the subject Delete my Radar account. Sending from the account address is how the request is verified — a request from any other address cannot be acted on.
  3. Include your Radar username in the message, so the right account is matched. If you signed in with Google, say so.
  4. Wait for the confirmation reply. You will get an email confirming the request within 7 days, and a second one once the deletion is done.

Email a deletion request

Signing out of Radar does not delete anything, and neither does uninstalling the app — your library is stored under your account and will still be there next time you sign in. Deletion has to be requested here.

What gets deleted

Everything below is erased from the database, permanently:

Data Deleted
Your account itself — email address, stored password hash, Google sign-in link Yes
Profile — username, display name, profile picture, favourites Yes
Your library — every title, format, status, rating, note, price, date added, watch history entry and episode you ticked off Yes
Activity entries, and the reactions and comments you left Yes
Friendships and any pending friend requests, in both directions Yes
Your notification inbox Yes
Settings — visibility, region, streaming services, notification and streak choices Yes
Every monthly and yearly Recap generated for you Yes
Push notification tokens for every device you signed in on Yes
Your public shelf link, which stops working immediately Yes

Nothing is kept for advertising, profiling or resale, because Radar does none of those things. There is no archived copy of your library after deletion.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Database backups — up to 30 further days. Routine backups taken before the deletion still contain your rows until they age out, which happens within 30 days of the deletion. They are not readable by the app and are never restored selectively.
  • The deletion request email — up to 12 months. Your message and the replies to it are kept as the record that the request was made and honoured, then deleted.
  • Web server logs — up to 30 days. Firebase Hosting keeps standard request logs for the web app, including IP addresses. They are not tied to your account and expire on their own.
  • Nothing else. No shadow profile, no anonymised copy of your watch history, no retained identifiers.

Deletion itself is completed within 30 days of a verified request, and usually much sooner.

Deleting only some of your data

If you want to keep the account but clear what is in it, you do not need to write to anyone. Removing a title from your library in the app deletes it and its watch history, and Settings → Profile visibility → Only Me hides your library, activity and friends list from everyone else without deleting anything. To wipe the library but keep the account, say so in the email instead of asking for full deletion.

Questions

Write to michal.dakowicz2007@gmail.com. What Radar stores and why is set out in the privacy policy.
